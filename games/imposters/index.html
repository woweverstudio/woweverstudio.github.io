A local party game

Imposters

A single-device social deduction game for three to ten people. Everyone shares clues. Someone has a different word.

App StorePrivacy policy

The twist

Fool Mode

Every player sees a word. The Imposters each receive a different one — and do not know they are the Imposter until the vote.

How a round works

Pass. Speak. Vote.

  1. 01

    Set the table

    Choose player count, category, game mode, and a discussion time.

  2. 02

    Keep your clue quiet

    Pass the iPhone around so each player can privately see their word or role.

  3. 03

    Leave a clue

    Take turns giving a short hint. Be useful enough for your team, vague enough to stay safe.

  4. 04

    Find the Imposter

    Vote together. If the Imposter is caught, they get one chance to guess the real word.
