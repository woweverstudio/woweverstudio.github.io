Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Imposters is made by woweverstudio. This policy explains how the app handles information when you play.

No personal data collection

Imposters does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The core game runs locally on your device and does not require an internet connection.

Game settings and custom content

Your game settings and any custom categories or words you create are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to woweverstudio.

In-app purchases

Imposters offers an optional non-consumable purchase that unlocks custom categories and words. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. woweverstudio does not receive your payment information.

Children

Imposters does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a revised date.

Contact
