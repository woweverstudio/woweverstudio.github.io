Guarantee rules

  • If no A or better appears in 9 pulls in a row, the 10th is guaranteed A or better (A 85% · S 15%). This counter resets to 0 whenever you get A or S.
  • If no S appears in 49 pulls in a row, the 50th is guaranteed S. Getting an S resets both counters to 0 (an A does not reset this one), and the counters never expire.
  • A 10-pull is resolved one pull at a time, checking guarantees on every pull. Every random opening with gems or keys counts.
