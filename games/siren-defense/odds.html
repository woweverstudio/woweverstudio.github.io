Guarantee rules

  • If no Epic+ appears in 9 pulls in a row, the 10th is guaranteed Epic+ (Epic 85% · Legendary 15%). This counter resets to 0 whenever you get Epic or better.
  • If no Legendary appears in 49 pulls in a row, the 50th is guaranteed Legendary. Getting a Legendary resets both counters to 0 (an Epic does not reset this one), and counters never expire.
  • A 10-pull is resolved one pull at a time, checking guarantees on every pull. Every random opening with gems or keys counts.
