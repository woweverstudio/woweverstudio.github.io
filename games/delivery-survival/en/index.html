Delivery Survival

Become a motorcycle courier and ride through a city full of zombies. Your gun fires on its own. You steer, deliver, and take down the boss that arrives after six minutes.

Free for iPhone and iPad. No ads, no in-app purchases.

6 minutes a run

Seoul and Okinawa

Ten tiers per city. Beat the boss to open the next tier and a new city.

How to play

Ride, deliver, beat the boss.

  1. 01

    Take the order

    Follow the arrow, pick up the food at the shop and bring it to the customer. Wait a moment inside the ring and the pickup or delivery is done.

  2. 02

    Just steer

    Put a thumb anywhere on the left side of the screen and drag to ride. Your gun shoots the nearest zombies by itself and gets stronger with every delivery.

  3. 03

    Repair shop and gas station

    Spend what you earn on a skill at the repair shop. Low fuel slows you down, so stop at a gas station.

  4. 04

    Beat the boss

    At six minutes a giant boss appears. Stay out of the red zones on the road and take it down to win.

Between runs

Earn more, go further.

01

Seven riders

From the Rookie Rider to Penguin Dash, Shark Surf Delivery and Baby Dragon Express. Each has its own speed, HP and attack.

02

Garage upgrades

Upgrade speed, HP and attack level by level. Upgrades apply to every rider.

03

Daily missions

Three new missions every day. Finish them and claim the rewards at Home.
